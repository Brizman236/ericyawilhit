About

Translations:
  • Fr

African digital sovereignty is a summit I’m setting out to climb. As a second-year undergraduate student, I’m not yet an expert, but I’m a passionate practitioner building, day by day, the building blocks of my future expertise.

My playground: the Labs. Convinced that theory alone isn’t enough, I’ve expanded my learning from PKI (certificate management) to IAM (Identity & Access Management). I document my progress by designing virtual architectures where I simulate real-world environments: directory centralization, SSO, MFA, and securing the identity lifecycle.

My vision, still under construction:

  • Learn the deep mechanics of digital trust.

  • Experiment with solutions like FreeIPA and Keycloak to understand “Identity Security” from the inside out.

  • Reflect, through my blog, on the strategic stakes of technological independence for the African continent.

I’m not just after a diploma — I’m after a technical and strategic mastery capable of serving the sovereignty of our nations tomorrow.

On this blog I publish articles on my thinking around digital sovereignty, on the challenges of an African PKI, and also on my architectures.
